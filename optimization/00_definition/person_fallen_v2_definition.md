# person_fallen V2.0 image-first definition

This event is defined by the current visible body state in a single image. A positive is a real person clearly lying, prone, supine, side-lying, horizontally collapsed, or curled in a lying state with substantial body support from a floor, ground, road, corridor, industrial/public-area surface, or other clearly abnormal non-rest support surface. Intentional lying on the ground is positive. A fall transition, cause, loss of balance, injury, consciousness, or temporal evidence is not required.

Negative covers ordinary standing, walking, sitting, work, and clearly normal rest surfaces such as beds and sofas. Hard negatives are explicit posture/support/depiction confounds such as ground sitting, kneeling, squatting, bending, exercise, crawling, mannequins, posters, screens, optical illusions, and normal bed/sofa lying designed as a visual confound. Uncertain is retained when posture or support cannot be reliably determined from the visible evidence.

The 500 inputs are AI-generated images from `batch_person-fallen-v2-camera1p5m`, with prompt-derived labels based on the user's confirmation that each image and corresponding prompt content are aligned. This is not independent human visual review and no VLM was used to create GT.
